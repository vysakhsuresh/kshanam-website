Help

Short answers. If yours is not here, see the bottom of the page.

What does it cost?

Nothing, at any point. There is no payment screen, no trial, and nothing locked behind an upgrade. You will never be asked for money to download your own invitation.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no password, because there is no server storing anything about you.

What happens to my photos?

They stay on your device. The video is made inside your browser, using your own phone or laptop to do the work. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to — this is a website made of files, with no database behind it.

What you type is remembered on your own device so a refresh does not lose it. Clearing your browser data clears it.

Is there a watermark?

No. Nothing is drawn over your invitation and there is no end card advertising us. What you make is yours.

Can I write in Malayalam, Hindi or Tamil?

Yes. Every line on every design is yours to change, and you can type in any script your phone's keyboard supports. The site's own text is in English for now, but what appears on the invitation is only ever what you type.

Can I see it before I make it?

That is the main thing this site does differently. The editor plays your finished invitation while you type, and shows every slide along the bottom with your own words already on them. Touch any field and it jumps to the slide that line appears on.

How do I send it on WhatsApp?

Press Send on WhatsApp on the ready screen. On most Android phones this hands the video straight to WhatsApp. If your browser cannot do that, press Download and attach the file in WhatsApp as you would any other video.

The video would not make. What now?

Making a video needs a reasonably recent browser. Chrome on Android and Safari on a current iPhone both work. If you see a message saying the format could not be used, try Chrome — the result will be more reliable.

Can I use my own music?

Yes, pick My own song and choose a file from your device. That is your choice and your responsibility. The music we provide was written for this site, so it is free of any copyright claim.

How is this free?

Hosting a site made of static files costs very little, and advertisements on the browsing pages cover it. There are none in the editor, none while your video is being made, and none beside the download and share buttons. That line will not move.
