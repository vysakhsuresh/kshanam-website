A small studio, on purpose.

Kshanam is a photography and film studio in Kochi. Small enough that the people you speak to are the people who turn up on the day, and that is not an accident — it is the only arrangement in which anyone remembers your cousin’s name.

Why we do it this way

Photographs are not souvenirs. They are evidence.

A wedding photograph has a strange job. For about a year it is decoration — something to post, something to frame. After that it quietly becomes the only remaining record of what a room felt like, and of people who are not in the room any more.

That second job is the one we work for. It is why we photograph the grandmother at the edge of the crowd rather than asking her to come and stand in the middle of it. It is why we would rather have a slightly imperfect frame of something true than a technically flawless one of something arranged.

It is also why we are stubborn about a few unfashionable things: finishing every photograph by hand, keeping your files for years after you have stopped thinking about us, and writing your rights into the contract instead of leaving them to goodwill.

What we hold to

Six things we will not be talked out of.

  1. Nobody gets arranged

    Beyond a short set of portraits you have agreed to, we do not direct, pose or re-stage anything. If a moment has passed, it has passed, and we go and find the next one.

  2. The light decides the schedule

    We will argue, politely, for moving your portraits to the last hour of daylight. It is the one request we make, and it is the difference between good photographs and the ones you will actually print.

  3. Everything gets finished by hand

    No batch presets applied to nine hundred frames. Each photograph you receive has been looked at by a person, which is slower and is the reason the galleries take three weeks.

  4. Your family can download everything

    One link, full resolution, no expiry, no per-print charge. Photographs that live behind a paywall stop being looked at, and a photograph nobody looks at has failed.

  5. We say no when we are wrong for you

    Some couples want formal, posed, everybody-smiling wedding photography, and that is a completely legitimate thing to want. We are not the studio for it, and we would rather tell you that on the first call than take the booking.

  6. Your wedding is not our content

    We ask before anything goes online, and if the answer is no, nothing does — not a story, not a single frame. It is written into the contract rather than left to our discretion.

What we photograph besides weddings

Portraits, films, and people who make things.

Weddings are most of the year, but not all of it. We take portrait sittings most weeks — an unhurried ninety minutes, usually outdoors, usually at the end of the day, and usually for people who are convinced they photograph badly.

We also work with makers, restaurants and small brands who need their actual room and their actual product photographed rather than a styled approximation of it. The approach is the same: available light where it will carry, one light where it will not, and no attempt to make the place look like somewhere else.
