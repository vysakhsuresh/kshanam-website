How we work

We stay out of the way, and we stay until the end.

Most of what you will want to look at later happens while nobody is performing: your father practising his speech to the mirror, a cousin asleep in a plastic chair, the ten seconds after the ceremony when everyone exhales at once.

So we photograph like documentary people. We learn the running order, we work out where the light will be at four in the afternoon, and then we get quiet. There is a short set of portraits if you want them — usually twenty minutes near the end of the day, when the light is worth the interruption. Everything else, we simply watch for.

And we stay. The last hour of a wedding, when the formal photographer has packed up and gone home, is routinely the best hour of the day.

What happens after you write to us

Four steps, no sales call.

  1. You send the date

    The date, roughly where, and anything you are already worried about. We reply within two working days with real availability and a real number.

  2. We talk properly

    Half an hour on a call or over coffee. We go through the running order, the rooms, the light, and who matters. No slideshow, no upselling.

  3. The day itself

    We arrive early, find the light, and then mostly disappear. You will forget we are there, which is the entire point.

  4. What you get back

    A private gallery your whole family can download from, within three weeks. Films within eight. Print rights in writing, so nobody has to ask us for permission later.
