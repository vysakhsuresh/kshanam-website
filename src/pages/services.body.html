The things that should never be extras.

Some studios charge for these. We think charging for them tells you something about how the rest of the relationship will go.

  • A pre-wedding call. Half an hour on the running order, the rooms and the light. The single biggest thing that improves the photographs.
  • Travel anywhere in Kerala. No per-kilometre line, no fuel surcharge.
  • A private gallery with family downloads. One link, everybody can take full-resolution files, no expiry date.
  • Print and personal-use rights in writing. You never have to come back and ask our permission to use a photograph of your own wedding.
  • Two sets of backups from the day itself. Cards are copied to two drives before anyone sleeps, and we keep your files for five years.
