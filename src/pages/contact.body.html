Before you write

Two things worth knowing.

Dates in the wedding season go early

Between November and February most Saturdays are gone several months ahead. If your date is in that window it is worth writing now and deciding later — we hold nothing without a deposit, but we will tell you honestly how fast a particular weekend is moving.

We would rather be the wrong studio in writing

If what you are describing is not what we do well, the reply will say so and name someone we think does it better. That is a worse business strategy and a much better way to spend a wedding day.
